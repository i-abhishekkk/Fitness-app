💪 MUSCLE-UP LADDER
1
10 Clean Pull-ups
Dead hang, full extension. No kipping. Chin clearly over bar.
10 reps · Weeks 1–6
2
Scapular Pull-ups (Dead-Hang Shrugs) ⚡ NEW
Hang dead from bar. WITHOUT bending elbows, shrug shoulder blades DOWN and together. This fires the serratus + lower trap — the exact neuro pathway for strict muscle-up initiation. Do these FIRST every Pull Day.
3×8 every Pull Day · Weeks 1–ongoing
3
Dip Strength (10+ reps)
Without 10 strong dips, the transition fails every time.
10 strict dips · Weeks 1–4
4
Explosive Chest-to-Bar Pull-ups ⚡ NEW
Max acceleration from dead hang. Pull so explosively your chest touches the bar. This is the exact power burst that carries you through the muscle-up transition. No kipping — pure vertical speed. Log reps as "C2B" in session tracker.
5 C2B reps · Weeks 4–8
5
Negative Muscle-up
Jump to top, lower through entire movement slowly. Builds connective tissue.
5 slow negatives · Weeks 6–10
6
Hollow-Body Bar Hangs + L-Sit Pull-up Regressions ⚡ NEW
Hang from bar in perfect hollow body (ribs closed, pelvis tilted, abs braced). Then progress to L-sit hold while hanging — legs parallel to floor. This trains the strict body position required for the clean muscle-up and eliminates hip-swing compensation. Do during skill sessions or as warm-up on Pull Days.
30s hollow hang → 10s L-sit hang · Weeks 4–12
7
First Muscle-up
Slight kip is legitimate. Go explosive, get hips to bar, transition wrists, press out.
1 clean rep · Months 3–5
8
Strict Muscle-up
No swing, no kip. Pure power. Elite tier calisthenics.
3 strict · Months 5–8
